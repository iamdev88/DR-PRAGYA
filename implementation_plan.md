# Implementation Plan - Sai Dental and Cosmetology Center Website

Build a complete, premium, production-grade website for **Sai Dental and Cosmetology Center** (Dr. Pragya Shrivastava) in Ratnagiri, Maharashtra.

## Design Philosophy & Tech Stack
- **Tech Stack**: HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 (modern custom properties, fluid typography, responsive layout grid), and modular JavaScript (`clinic-data.js`, `app.js`).
- **Typography**: Google Fonts Inter + Cormorant Garamond / Playfair Display / Manrope for editorial elegance.
- **Color Palette**: Deep Charcoal (`#121619`), Warm Ivory / Linen (`#FAF8F5`, `#F4F0EA`), Muted Rose / Refined Champagne Sand (`#C5A880`, `#D4A373`, subtle rose undertone `#D9A7A0`), Soft warm slate (`#71767B`), Pristine White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Mobile First & Conversion Focused**: Sticky action bar (Call, WhatsApp, Book Consultation), floating WhatsApp trigger, interactive Appointment Request modal with pre-filled WhatsApp action and clear disclosure notices.
- **Data-Driven Architecture**: Centralized `clinic-data.js` containing verified clinic details, phone, address, ratings, hours, editable service structures, and doctor credentials.

## Sections & Key Features
1. **Header**: Sticky glass-morphism header, branding, navigation, direct Call & Appointment CTAs, elegant mobile drawer.
2. **Hero Section**: Refined typography, dual Dental + Cosmetology focus, Google 5.0 Rating trust chip, direct contact actions.
3. **Trust Strip**: 5.0 Google Rating (27 verified reviews), Cosmetic Dentistry, Ratnagiri, Maharashtra location badge.
4. **Meet Dr. Pragya Shrivastava**: Professional doctor profile layout with clearly labeled editable qualification placeholders (avoiding medical fabrication) and consultation philosophy.
5. **Care Philosophy & About the Clinic**: Aesthetic precision, gentle clinical experience, modern dental & aesthetic care.
6. **Services Architecture**: Comprehensive categorized grid with general, cosmetic, smile enhancement, preventive, restorative, and cosmetology placeholders.
7. **Cosmetic Dentistry Feature**: Spotlight on cosmetic consultation, smile aesthetics, veneers, and teeth whitening consultation cards.
8. **Cosmetology Suite**: Aesthetics and skin health consultation placeholders.
9. **Why Choose Us**: 4-pillar value system (Personalized Consultation, Aesthetic Approach, Patient Comfort, Ratnagiri Accessibility).
10. **The Patient Journey**: 4 clear, anxiety-reducing steps (Book -> Consult -> Plan -> Begin).
11. **Google Reviews & Patient Trust**: Verified 5.0 rating presentation with verified reviews count and Google Maps profile redirect.
12. **Location & Clinic Accessibility**: Full address at Sai Hospital, TRP Road, MIDC Mirjole, Ratnagiri, Plus code `X8WR+HP`, map view, directions button, and verified clinic hours.
13. **Appointment & Consultation Booking Form**: Intuitive form with WhatsApp direct forwarding option, date/time pickers, and clear feedback.
14. **Footer & Compliance**: Full local SEO metadata, schema markup (`LocalBusiness`, `Dentist`), Agency credit (Designed & Developed by YSH&CO).
